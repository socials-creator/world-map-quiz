How a round works

Tap the country you're shown. A round covers all ~197 UN member countries. You stay in one continent for a bit, then occasionally jump somewhere new. Miss or skip one, and the map pans to show you where it was. End the round by finding every country, or after 5 misses — whichever comes first.

Map legend

Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Aksai Chin, Shaksgam Valley, and Pakistan-administered Kashmir are shown grouped with India's color but with distinct borders, and aren't part of the quiz — borders here are simplified reference lines, not a legal boundary.
