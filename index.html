How a round works

Tap the country you're shown. A round keeps you exploring one continent, then occasionally jumps you somewhere new. It ends when you've placed every country — or after 5 misses, whichever comes first. Miss one or skip it, and the map will pan in and point to where it actually was. Pinch or scroll to zoom in — small countries like Portugal or the Caribbean and Pacific islands get much easier to tap once you're zoomed in.

Map legend

Disputed regions (e.g. Aksai Chin, Pakistan-administered Kashmir) are shown in grey and aren't part of the quiz — borders here are simplified reference lines, not a political statement.
